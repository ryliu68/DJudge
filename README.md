# DJudge: How Far Are We? Assessing the Discrepancies Between AI-synthesized Images and Natural Images through Multimodal Guidance

## Introduction

This repository contains the PyTorch implementation for the paper "D-Judge： How Far Are We? Assessing the Discrepancies Between AI-synthesized Images and Natural Images through Multimodal Guidance".


## Abstract

In Artificial Intelligence Generated Content (AIGC), distinguishing AI-synthesized images from natural ones remains a key challenge. Despite advancements in generative models, significant discrepancies persist. 
To systematically investigate and quantify these discrepancies, we introduce an AI-Natural Image Discrepancy accessing benchmark (D-Judge) aimed at addressing the critical question: *how far are AI-generated images (AIGIs) from truly realistic images?* We construct D-ANI, a dataset with 5,000 natural images and over 440,000 AIGIs generated by nine models using Text-to-Image (T2I), Image-to-Image (I2I), and Text and Image-to-Image (TI2I) prompts. Our framework evaluates the discrepancy across five dimensions: naive image quality, semantic alignment, aesthetic appeal, downstream applicability, and human validation. Results reveal notable gaps, emphasizing the importance of aligning metrics with human judgment. 

## Dataset

**We have released the D-ANI dataset on [Hugging Face Hub](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Renyang/DANI) for public research use.**

- Direct link: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/Renyang/DANI](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Renyang/DANI)

The dataset contains over 445,000 images, including natural and AI-generated samples, with comprehensive metadata for advanced visual research.
